## 2. Data we process

**Account**  
First name, last name, email address, phone number, and password. The password is stored as a hash, not in a form we can read.

**Booking**  
Locker size, start and end time, locker access codes, and the email or SMS messages used to send those codes.

**Payment**  
Payment method: card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay, through Stripe. Stripe processes the card or wallet details. We receive the payment status and a transaction reference. We do not store the full card number or the CVV.

**Use of the app and website**  
IP address, device type, and basic technical logs needed to keep you signed in, to run the service, and to protect the account against abuse. We do not use advertising cookies and we do not build profiles for advertising.

**Video surveillance**  
A video-surveillance system operates at the locker premises. It records image and sound, to protect customers, luggage, staff, and the premises. A notice at the entrance states that image and sound are recorded.

**Messages you send us**  
What you send by email, through the account-deletion form, or through support channels (for example WhatsApp or Viber), so that we can answer the request.

We do not ask for an identity-card number, and we do not send promotional email, SMS, or push notifications. Our messages are only about the booking: confirmation, access codes, and necessary notices about that rental.

## 3. Why we process data, and the legal basis

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Create the account and rent the locker | Account and booking details | Contract |
| Send access codes by email and SMS | Email, phone number, codes | Contract |
| Take payment | Data exchanged with Stripe | Contract |
| Invoices and tax records | Transaction details | Legal obligation |
| Account security and abuse prevention | IP address, technical logs, booking history | Legitimate interest |
| Video surveillance of the premises | Image and sound from the system | Legitimate interest |
| Answer a support or deletion request | What you send us | Contract or legitimate interest |
| Respond to an authority when the law requires it | The data the authority asks for | Legal obligation |

Our legitimate interest is the safe operation of the lockers and the protection of customers and the premises. We do not use the data for advertising.

The locker is released automatically when the 24-hour booking ends. That follows the end time of the rental. It is not profiling, and it is not an automated decision with legal effects beyond performing the rental.

We aim for our providers to process data inside the European Economic Area.

Stripe and some infrastructure providers may also process data outside the EEA, including in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer relies on a European Commission adequacy decision or on Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 6. How long we keep data

- **Account:** for as long as the account is active. After a deletion request, we delete the account data, except records we must keep for a legal claim or a tax duty.
- **Bookings and invoices:** at least five (5) years from the end of the relevant tax year, or longer if the law requires it.
- **Locker codes:** for the rental period and, after that, as part of the booking record.
- **Image and sound:** up to fifteen (15) days, then deleted. If there is an incident (damage, theft, or a dispute), the relevant clip is kept for as long as needed to investigate it or to bring or defend a claim, and is then deleted.
- **Support messages:** for as long as needed to close the request, and for a reasonable period afterwards if the same matter continues.

## 7. Your rights

You may ask us for:

- access to your data
- correction of inaccurate data
- erasure
- restriction of processing
- objection to processing based on legitimate interest
- portability of the data you gave us and that we process by automated means in order to perform the contract

We reply within one month. If a request is complex, the period may be extended by two months, and we will tell you.

To protect the account, we may ask you to confirm that the request comes from you.

You may lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, Kifisias 1–3, 115 23 Athens, Greece, www.dpa.gr. We would like you to write to us first, so we can try to resolve it directly.

## 8. Account deletion

You can request deletion:

- in the app, from the account settings, or
- on the form at https://www.locknleave.gr/delete-account, using the account email

We delete the account data. We keep invoices and transaction records for as long as tax and accounting law requires. An active rental must first be completed or expire.

## 9. Cookies

On the website we use only cookies and similar storage that are strictly necessary for the page to work, to remember the language, and to keep the session secure. We do not use advertising cookies or marketing analytics cookies.

Because these cookies are strictly necessary, we do not ask for separate consent for them.

## 10. Security

We take measures appropriate to the risk: access control on our systems, hashed passwords, and payments through Stripe so that card details are not stored on our own servers.

No system connected to the internet is completely secure. If an incident seriously risks your data, we will inform you when the law requires it.

## 11. Age

The service is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect children’s data. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we delete it.

## 12. Changes to this Policy

If the way we process data changes, we publish the new Policy on the website and in the app and we change the date at the top. Where the law requires fresh notice before a change, we give that notice.
